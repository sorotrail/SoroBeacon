Scopes

{{range .Scopes}} {{end}}

A token can only be given scopes the signed-in credential already has. monitors:write creates, edits, deletes and imports monitors; channels:write includes sending a test notification; tokens:write mints and revokes tokens, and is how a credential could widen itself — grant it deliberately.
